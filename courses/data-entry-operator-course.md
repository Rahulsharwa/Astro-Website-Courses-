# Data Entry Operator Course

## Build Speed, Accuracy and Office-Ready Data Skills

Learn typing, spreadsheet data entry, record management, formatting, verification, and back-office workflows through practical training.

## What You Will Learn

- Typing, data-entry software, and spreadsheet entry
- Data formatting, sorting, filtering, and record organization
- Data verification and correction of common errors
- File and document management
- Workplace-oriented administrative tasks

- **Location:** Karmchari Bhawan, Dhamtari

- **Typing practice:** 1 hour
- **Computer facility:** One computer for each student
- **Age limit:** No age limit
- **Admission:** Direct admission at the institute
- **Course fee:** ₹4,000

## Before Joining

- Slow or inaccurate typing, limited spreadsheet and data-entry knowledge, and difficulty managing large amounts of information.

## After Completing the Course

- Improved typing speed and accuracy, ability to enter and organize records, sort and filter data, verify information, correct errors, and work in data-entry and back-office roles.

## Benefits and Facilities

- Experienced faculty, individual computer access, practical data-entry exercises, typing tests, accuracy guidance, assessments, doubt-solving, career guidance, job-oriented training, and certificate support after course completion.
