# DCA (Diploma in Computer Applications)

## Build Job-Ready Computer Skills

DCA is a practical diploma course for students, job seekers, and working professionals who want a strong foundation in computer applications and office productivity.

## What You Will Learn

- Computer fundamentals, hardware, software, and operating systems
- Windows, file management, internet, email, and online services
- MS Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
- Typing and document formatting
- Basic database and presentation concepts
- Cyber safety, digital payments, and everyday computer use

## Course Highlights

- Beginner-friendly, hands-on training
- Practical assignments and guided projects
- Certificate on successful completion
- Suitable for office, clerical, education, and entry-level roles

## Recommended For

Students, beginners, homemakers, job seekers, and anyone preparing for computer-based exams or office work.
