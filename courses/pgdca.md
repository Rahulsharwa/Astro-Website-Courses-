# PGDCA (Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applications)

## Advance Your Career in Computer Applications

PGDCA is designed for graduates and learners who want deeper knowledge of software, programming, databases, and professional IT applications.

## What You Will Learn

- Computer architecture, operating systems, and networking fundamentals
- Programming logic and application development basics
- Database management and SQL concepts
- Web technology and internet applications
- Office automation and data handling
- Software engineering, project planning, and documentation
- Practical project work and presentation skills

## Course Highlights

- Structured, career-oriented curriculum
- Practical labs and project-based learning
- Strong foundation for entry-level IT and office technology roles
- Certificate on successful completion

## Recommended For

Graduates, diploma holders, professionals upgrading their skills, and learners preparing for IT-related opportunities.
