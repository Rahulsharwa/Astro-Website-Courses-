# Practical Computer Training

## Learn by Doing

Practical Computer Training focuses on the everyday tasks people need at school, work, business, and home—with guided practice at every step.

- Printing, scanning, PDF handling, and document sharing

- Online safety, passwords, backups, and troubleshooting
