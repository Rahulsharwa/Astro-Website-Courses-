# Tally Prime / ERP 9

## Master Computerized Accounting

Learn to manage accounts, billing, inventory, taxation, and business records using Tally Prime and ERP 9 in a practical classroom environment.

## What You Will Learn

- Company creation, ledgers, groups, and vouchers
- Sales, purchase, receipt, payment, and journal entries
- GST basics, invoicing, and tax-related reports
- Inventory, stock groups, units, and godowns
- Banking, reconciliation, and financial reports
- Profit and loss, balance sheet, and trial balance
- Payroll and practical accounting workflows

## Course Highlights

- Guided exercises and assessment support
- Certificate on successful completion
