## Diploma & Professional Courses

- **DCA (Diploma in Computer Applications)** - Rs 12,650 + examination fee. Eligibility: 10th pass.
  Computer fundamentals and operating systems, MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint, typing, internet
  and email, file and document management, basic databases, presentations and cyber safety.
  Affiliated to Dr. C.V. Raman University, Bilaspur.

- **PGDCA (Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applications)** - Rs 15,650 + examination fee.
  Eligibility: 12th pass. Computer architecture and operating systems, programming logic,
  database management and SQL, web technology and internet applications, software engineering
  and project work. Affiliated to Dr. C.V. Raman University, Bilaspur.

- **Tally Prime** - Rs 5,000. Company creation, groups and ledgers, sales/purchase/receipt/
  payment and journal vouchers, billing, inventory and stock management, GST-related accounting
  practice, banking, reconciliation and financial reports.

- **AI Course** - Rs 5,000. AI fundamentals and responsible use, writing effective prompts,
  AI for documents, research and presentations, content and idea generation workflows,
  privacy, fact-checking, copyright and safe AI practice.

- **Data Entry Operator** - Rs 4,000. Typing technique and accuracy, spreadsheet data entry,
  record organisation and file management, formatting, sorting, filtering and checking,
  practical office workflows.

## Computer Skills & Office Courses

- **Basic Computer Course** - Fee on enquiry. Computer parts and operating systems, keyboard and
  mouse confidence, files, folders and storage, internet, email and online services, printing,
  scanning, PDFs and online safety.

- **MS Office (Word, Excel & PowerPoint)** - Fee on enquiry. Word letters, reports, tables,
  formatting and PDF export; Excel data entry, formulas, functions, charts and filters;
  PowerPoint slides, layouts, images, charts and animations; workplace templates and
  practical assignments; file management, printing and document sharing.

- **Typing Course** - Rs 500 per month. Correct finger placement and touch-typing technique,
  progressive speed-building drills, accuracy review and error-pattern correction, timed
  typing tests and progress tracking.

- **Hindi & English Typing** - Rs 500 per month. English keyboard technique and speed drills,
  Hindi typing layouts and language input tools, switching between Hindi and English,
  bilingual document and form preparation, timed practice for typing tests.

- **Practical Computer Training** - Fee on enquiry, depending on modules selected. Operating
  systems and device setup, word processing, spreadsheets and presentations, internet, email
  and online forms, printing, scanning and PDF handling, data entry, typing, online safety
  and basic troubleshooting.

## Batch Timings

- 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM
- 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM

Training format: 1 hour theory and 1 hour practical, with one computer for each student.
Fixed batch timings are currently listed for DCA and PGDCA; other course schedules are
confirmed at admission. Typing practice may be scheduled as a separate one-hour session.

## Admission

Direct admission at the institute. No age limit. For DCA bring your 10th marksheet; for PGDCA
bring 10th and 12th marksheets. Both also need an Aadhaar Card photocopy and passport-size
photographs.

Marksheet and certificate facilities are provided according to eligibility, course rules and
applicable affiliation guidelines. Fees, batch availability and course duration should be
confirmed with the institute.
